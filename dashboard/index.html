How these numbers are counted
  • Within your rules: only the resting orders the practice trader chose, at the sizes your draft limits allow ($10 per side, $5 for player props, $40 in use per platform).
  • Full-size paper test: every order the simulator planned, at full size. It shows how the strategy behaves, not what your $100 would do.
  • Realized: positions whose game is over and whose markets have settled. Unrealized: hedged profit that's locked in but not settled yet.
  • Open risk: contracts that filled but couldn't be hedged. They count as a total loss until their game settles.
  • A resting order counts as filled only within 15 minutes of being placed, and the hedge is priced at the worse of the order book and the price at the minute of the fill.
  • Nothing here is real money. The bot builds the orders it would send but sends none.
